# WiNGHacks The Dumpling Merchant
A narrative-driven tapping game created for WingHacks 2026. Follow the journey of a hesitant merchant struggling with their family's dumpling business as they seek the legendary Horse of the Lunar Year to find the courage to break free from their limitations.

# 🏮 The Story
Throughout centuries, the main character has been in a family that makes dumplings to serve the Jade Emperor. Despite their skill, they feel trapped by tradition and hesitant about their future. Since it is the Lunar Year of the Horse, representing strength, speed, and the breaking of boundaries, the merchant sets out to discover themselves and meet various Zodiac animals. One of them being the Fire Horse itself, which provides unexpected guidance to the merchant's concerns.

# 🛠️ Built With
**Engine**: Godot 4.x

# Language: GDScript

# Art: Custom Pixel Art

# 🥟 Game Mechanics: The Dumpling Minigame
The core gameplay centers on a rhythmic tapping system that mirrors the preparation of traditional dumplings:

**Add Ingredients**: Tap to add the correct ratio of meat, vegetables, and seasoning.

**The Fold**: Tap in sequence to seal the dumpling.

**Finish**: Press the Finish Button to complete the batch and serve the customer.

# ✨ Key Features
**Narrative Choice**: Engage in dialogue with Zodiac animals like the cat, rat, and rooster to explore the merchant's internal struggle.

**Animated World**: Features a lively background with spirited background music that celebrate the Lunar New Year theme.

**Atmospheric Visuals**: Dynamic scenes including a pixel-art sunset main menu and an interactive "End Credits" scene featuring the Zodiac animals.

**Symbolic Journey**: Progressing towards something you are unsure about is always frightening, however this game shows that you need to take risks in order to break past the boundaries.

# 🚀 How to Play
**Start**: Launch the game and select Start Game from the main menu.

**Interact**: Talk to the celestial Zodiacs and gain insight on different perspectives about traditions.

**Minigame**: Memorize the Zodiacs's dumpling orders to create their dumplings.
